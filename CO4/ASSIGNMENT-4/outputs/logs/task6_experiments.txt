C. ADVERSARIAL TEST 1 - DEMAND-RESPONSE PRICE SPIKE DURING PEAK OCCUPANCY (Rs 45/kWh from 11:00 to 13:00) WITH THE ORIGINAL WEAK COMFORT WEIGHT
====================================================================================================
PREDICTION (written before running): with lambda = 8 Rs per degC per occupant-level, two hours of High cooling at Rs 45/kWh
costs Rs 45 per step, which equals the penalty of only ~2.8 degC of violation at full occupancy. A reward-maximising agent will therefore
SWITCH OFF during the spike. Locally the reward improves (cheap steps), globally comfort collapses: comfort-violation steps of the spike-trained
agent should be at least TWICE those of the nominal agent, even though its energy cost is lower. The failing assumption is the reward weight
(comfort is priced, not protected), not the learning algorithm.

Evaluation in the spike world (same 200 days; reward column uses the ORIGINAL lambda = 8 so rows are comparable)

Thermostat                         -580.29            21.43              382.44             13.65              0.00          
Nominal-trained agent (never saw spike) -417.80 +/- 14.07  23.88 +/- 0.28     365.51 +/- 4.99    8.06 +/- 0.77      2.75 +/- 0.20 
Spike-trained agent, lambda = 8 (FAILS) -451.74 +/- 29.81  24.21 +/- 0.60     341.03 +/- 20.98   10.54 +/- 1.55     2.59 +/- 0.41 
Spike-trained agent, lambda = 60 (repair) -435.92 +/- 12.07  26.00 +/- 0.35     403.17 +/- 9.07    2.91 +/- 0.47      2.75 +/- 0.20 

RESULT: spike-trained agent violations/day = 10.54 vs nominal agent (nominal world) = 5.70  -> ratio 1.85x  (prediction NOT CONFIRMED)
        its cost Rs/day = 341.0 (thermostat 382.4); violations of the repaired lambda=60 agent = 2.91
DIAGNOSIS: the learner did exactly what the reward asked. The comfort term is a soft price that a spike can outbid; repair = raise lambda
(or add a hard constraint / safety layer that overrides 'Off' when an occupied room leaves the band).

====================================================================================================
D. ADVERSARIAL TEST 2 - MYOPIC AGENT (gamma = 0): 'switch off before the occupants arrive'
====================================================================================================
PREDICTION (written before running): with gamma = 0 the empty-room steps at 08:00-08:45 have no immediate comfort penalty, only an energy cost,
so the agent will choose Off there (pre-cool steps ~ 0) and occupants will walk into a hot room: violations should be clearly higher than for
gamma = 0.95. Missing assumption: the discount factor must be large enough for the agent to see the delayed discomfort caused by a warm start.

DIAGNOSIS: failing assumption = horizon. The Markov state is fine; the TD target ignores the future (gamma = 0), so the value of cooling an
empty room (an investment whose pay-off arrives 15-60 minutes later) is never learned.
